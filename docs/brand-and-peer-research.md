## A shared look across platforms

- Use **Quant at Illinois** as the written name, **@quantillinois** as the social handle, and the existing Quant wordmark or standalone mark on branded assets.
- Use paper **#F7F5EF** and ink **#142433** for most surfaces and copy. Use blue **#225C7B** for links and labels, mist **#DCEBF0** for pale backgrounds, and deep blue **#102B40** for competition features. These colors carry the Instagram reference into the site.
- Keep the original animated contour field as the website’s signature. Use its linework as a still motif for social covers and retain a static version on the site for reduced-motion settings.
- Pair compact sans-serif headlines with small, spaced labels. Let the logo supply the serif detail. Keep gradients behind photography or open space, and check contrast where type sits over color.
- Use real campus and club photography. For social covers, use a short title and a consistent category label such as **Recruitment**, **Event recap**, or **Meet the team**. Apply the same label when the update appears on the website or LinkedIn.
- Use the website for durable information and competition recaps, Discord for recruiting announcements and logistics, and Instagram and LinkedIn for event highlights. Link back to a single source when publishing a deadline or registration form.

Before a new recruiting cycle, confirm the roster, sponsor tiers, and active application links with the board. The site carries forward the existing roster and sponsor list and directs students to Discord for announcements.
